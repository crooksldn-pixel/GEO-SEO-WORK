# Crooks LDN: UK GEO baseline, 16 Oct 2026

This is the first measured run. It covers 21 queries: Google UK once each, and ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity 3 runs each in fresh chats. It was collected from a UK (GB) connection in a signed-out browser between 20:28 and 22:03 BST. All counts come from `results.csv`. The old 43→54 scores are not used.

**Gaps:** Perplexity hit a sign-up wall after 5 answers, so 58 of its 63 runs are blocked. The measuring Mac went offline before 5 Google UK queries could run: Corteiz alternatives, brands like Carsicko, brands like Broken Planet, water reactive clothing and heat reactive clothing. Those rows are marked NOT RUN.

- **Crooks only appears when the query contains its name.** It was named in none of the 110 readable non-brand AI answers and in none of the 13 non-brand Google results pages that ran (top 20 and AI Overview).
- **Brand answers are mostly accurate but not always the right entity.** They correctly give the Bourne End returns office, 550gsm Convict fleece, Yard denim, and Trustpilot at about 4.1/5 (shipping delays are the main complaint). The entity misses were:
  - ChatGPT "Crooks London" run 1 described *Crooks 1926*, a theatre show.
  - ChatGPT run 2 led with *Crooks' Corner* pub.
  - Perplexity's "Crooks LDN" sources included a **Crooks & Castles** Trustpilot page (confirmed in runs 2–3; run 1 also lists fr.trustpilot.com).
  - Two Gemini brand answers were ungrounded (no sources).
- **On specialist queries, no AI answer names any brand for water-reactive clothing.**
  - ChatGPT asked clarifying questions in 6 of 6 runs.
  - Gemini explained hydrochromic print, citing textile makers (one run only said "I cannot fulfill this request").
  - Heat-reactive puffer answers named Remyx London, Mertra, PESO, Trapstar and Pretty Boy Ugly World.
