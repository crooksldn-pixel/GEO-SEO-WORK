## Inputs

- **FX:** mid-market rates implied by Shopify's own live conversions on 18 Oct (converted price ÷ 1.02): USD 1.334, EUR 1.186, JPY 210.5, AUD 1.922, CAD 1.886, IDR 24,025. The script reproduces every current Shopify price from these rates.
- **Shopify Payments currency conversion fee:** 2% (all regions except the US and France).
- **Card fees, Grow plan (UK):** 1.7% + 25p UK cards; 2.7% + 25p international cards (published UK rates — confirm in your Shopify Payments terms).
- **Cost-covered value:** GBP × FX × 1.03 (2% conversion + 1% international card premium).
- **UK net:** GBP price minus 1.7% + 25p. Net for fixed local prices: local ÷ FX minus 2% conversion minus 2.7% + 25p. Shipping, product cost and duty excluded (shipping is in the shipping matrix; unit costs aren't in Shopify).
- **Exception rule:** if the next point up lifts net more than 10% above UK and the point below is within 3%, take the point below (⚑).
