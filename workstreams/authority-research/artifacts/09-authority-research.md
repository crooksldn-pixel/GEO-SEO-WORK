**Rules that apply.** These come from Trustpilot's Guidelines for Businesses, v7.3, Sept 2026 (https://corporate.trustpilot.com/legal/for-businesses/guidelines-for-businesses/sep-2026):
- "Invite consistently and fairly". Positive and negative experiences must be treated the same way.
- No screening, i.e. sending happy customers to review and unhappy ones to support.
- "We don't allow incentivized reviews on Trustpilot": no discounts, codes, freebies, prize draws or refunds in connection with a review.
- No reviews from "someone who is working for your business", immediate family or competitors.
- Misuse can lead to a Consumer Warning on the profile and a hidden TrustScore.
