## Notes for other workstreams

**A. Brand-owned "best brands" guides are among the most-cited sources.**
- Phrase Studios (25 citations), Cali Studios (11), Flow Like Zen, CUOTTI and Rosenberries are all brands citing their own lists. Three of them get named in AI answers because of those lists.
- That makes a CROOKSLDN-owned guide a GEO lever. It belongs to the content/collections workstream and is subject to the "one URL per intent, no new landing pages" rule.
- If it's ever done, it must disclose ownership (e.g. "CROOKSLDN is our own brand"; the off---record.com page is the clean example). It must also rank peers honestly. A self-ranking list without disclosure is the pattern to avoid.

**B. AI assistants tie the brand to a different company than the profiles will.**
- Perplexity (3/3 brand runs) and ChatGPT (1 run) cite Companies House for **Crooks London Limited (16396366)**. That's incorporated 19 Apr 2025 with an Englefield Green registered office, and George is its sole active director (checked on Companies House 18 Oct 2026).
- The prepared LinkedIn and Crunchbase copy names **Crooks Clothing Company Ltd (16606021)**, as George instructed.
- George needs to decide which entity is the brand's public entity. Once the profiles go live, the two will diverge across Companies House, Crunchbase and LinkedIn unless one is clearly named as the operator.

**C. "London" descriptors.**
- ChatGPT asked "Are you looking for CROOKSLDN, the London streetwear brand?" in 3 of the brand runs.
- Perplexity described it as a London label in all 3 brand runs.
- This supports FINDINGS 6: the contact page's "run out of London" copy is one of the signals feeding it.

**D. Trustpilot is the review source every assistant uses.**
- Perplexity quoted 4.1/5 from 79 reviews on 16 Oct. The live page shows 4.2 from 80.
- Perplexity's sources also pull in a Crooks & Castles Trustpilot page (fr.trustpilot.com).
- So the Trustpilot replies and fixes (FINDINGS 2–4) feed straight into AI brand answers. Renaming the profile to CROOKSLDN (§3.1) helps separate it from Crooks & Castles.
