Shipping & Delivery
We ship fast and keep you tracked the whole way.

What it costs. UK Royal Mail Tracked 48 is free from £30, and Tracked 24 is free from £80. Under that, Tracked 48 is £3 and Tracked 24 is £4.99. Shipping internationally? Add your items to your bag and the cost is worked out at checkout from weight and destination.

When it ships. In-stock pieces ordered by 18:00, Monday to Saturday, go out the same day; after that, on the next dispatch day. T-shirts are printed to order and dispatched within 2–3 working days. Limited drops and pre-orders ship on the date shown on the product page.

When it lands. After dispatch, UK delivery by Tracked 24 typically takes 1–2 working days and by Tracked 48 typically 2–3. International orders typically take 7–14 working days. These are estimated delivery aims, not guaranteed dates, and delivery can occasionally take longer. Tracking is emailed the moment your parcel leaves us.

Heading overseas? Any import duties your country charges on arrival are set by local customs and aren't included at checkout — just so there's no surprise on the doorstep.

Lost or damaged? That's on us to sort. Message us within 14 days and we'll chase the courier or send a replacement or refund — a quick photo helps for damage.

How: email team@crooksldn.com or DM @crooksldn with your order number. We reply within 1–2 working days (Mon–Sat). This doesn't affect your statutory rights.
