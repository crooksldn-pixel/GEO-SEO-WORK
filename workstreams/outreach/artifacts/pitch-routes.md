- **GUAP:** "Seeding and Paid Promotion" form (https://forms.monday.com/forms/0aea8f1e4dc523aae1ca7aae1301845a?r=use1).

The body lists "podcast interviews" among the exposure options. The guest pitch has to say plainly that we aren't buying a slot.

- **Ape to Gentleman:** the Advertise page (https://apetogentleman.com/advertise/) offers sponsored "In partnership with" posts.
- **The Sole Supplier and OPUMO:** the only published routes are advertising.
