## Disco (discobrands.co/submit)

Free. The page says "Free to apply", "No payment required" and "Approved listings are always free". Every application is reviewed by hand, usually within 5–7 days. If accepted, Disco builds the profile and emails the link. Image files must be PNG, JPG or WebP, under 5 MB. The button reads "Submit application →".
