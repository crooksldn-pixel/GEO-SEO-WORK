# Brand photo and video assets: what to upload and where

Upload here: drop files into `/mnt/project-files/seo/assets/incoming/` from a folder on your Mac, or attach them to a message in the Image search audit thread. Either works. Anything attached in the project also lands in `uploads/hearth/` and gets sorted from there.

Only use original media that Crooks owns: your own shoots, phone photos and Instagram/TikTok originals you shot. No supplier mock-ups, stock images or AI renders.

## File naming (helps whoever uploads them to Shopify)
`crooksldn-{product}-{colour}-{view}.jpg`, for example `crooksldn-hydrocuff-windbreaker-black-wet-front.jpg` or `crooksldn-grey-convict-hoodie-on-body-front.jpg`. JPEG or WebP is preferred over PNG for photos with a background. Keep the camera originals too.

For each on-body or reaction shot, add a line to `incoming/notes.md`: what it shows, the model's height and size worn, and for reaction shots what triggered it (water, body heat, hairdryer and so on). Per the 16 Oct brief, nothing about the trigger, timing, reversibility or wash durability goes on the site until that is documented from supplier or test data. The photos can go up before that; the technical claims wait.
