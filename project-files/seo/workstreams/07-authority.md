## Key findings

1. **Brand-discovery directories are the fastest GEO win.** Disco (discobrands.co) runs five or more "brands like Broken Planet / Trapstar", "hoodies like Trapstar" and "cheaper alternatives to Trapstar" pages updated in Jul–Oct 2026, with a free, human-reviewed "Submit your brand" form. Streetwear Directory's "brands like Corteiz but cheaper" page was re-verified 9 Oct 2026 and has an "Add a Brand" route (cost not stated). These are exactly the pages AI answers cite for the competitor queries.
2. **UK streetwear-native titles do cover small labels.** PAUSE (public submissions page; 12 Oct 2026 "15 Black British founders" list), GUAP (best British streetwear list that included Jehucal, Soho Yacht Club, GCLO Studios; asks readers to suggest brands), The Sole Supplier ("what is X" explainers on Syna in its first months), The Hoxton Trend, CULTED and Unruly.
3. **Heavyweight-hoodie buying guides leave out streetwear.** Fashion Beans (updated 18 Sep 2026, top result), Ape to Gentleman, OPUMO and Men's Flair list Asket, Wax London, CDLP, Arket; Represent and Nicce are usually the only street labels. A 550gsm hoodie is a credible product pitch there.
4. **Creators who already run the exact formats exist:** UK hoodie head-to-heads (No Sauce Hazza: Corteiz vs Trapstar vs Carsicko vs Syna), "underrated UK brands" spotlights (KEON), small-UK-label reviews (Josh Sullivan), "Is X worth it?" (Clothier) and founder podcasts for emerging London labels (Wears My Podcast / WMTV LDN; Menswear Style Podcast, which invites founder pitches).
5. **The big titles are a later stage.** Highsnobiety, Hypebeast and Complex's ranking lists are old or top-tier only. Complex UK's "Brand to Watch" column and i-D's "next big brands" format are the realistic way in, and both need a strong visual hook (the reactive outerwear) and a founder story.
6. **Entity facts are inconsistent, which blocks profiles.** Companies House has two candidate companies: CROOKSLDN LTD (16161993, [town redacted], inc. 3 Jan 2025) and CROOKS CLOTHING COMPANY LTD (16606021, [town redacted], inc. 24 Jul 2025). WS6 treats 16606021 as the verified trading entity (schema and Trustpilot use it); the [town redacted] company's link to the brand is unknown. The brand name says London. LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Disco and every pitch must use one agreed set of facts.
7. **No P0.** Nothing here is urgent or revenue-breaking. The one risk to watch is name confusion with Crooks & Castles and lookalike "official store" domains (seen for Corteiz).
