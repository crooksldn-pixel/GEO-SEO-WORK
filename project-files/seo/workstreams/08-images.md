## Current state in one paragraph
The technical side is already good: the product gallery and collection cards use responsive `srcset`/`sizes`, the first image is eager with `fetchpriority="high"`, everything else is lazy, Shopify's CDN serves WebP/AVIF at the requested width, product JSON-LD lists up to five images at 1600px plus a per-variant image, and og:image is served at 1200px. Alt text is descriptive on 21 of 25 products (the 16 Oct P1 audit flag about missing alt on denim second images is now fixed). The gaps are content, not code: one live product has no photo at all, the two reactive products (the most visually distinctive and highest-priced items) have the weakest image sets, three of four sets show only half the set, and no product has an on-body shot.

## Evidence notes
- **Theme code, live theme:** gallery `sections/crooks-exhibit-record.liquid` L150–185 (widths 400–1400, sizes `(min-width: 960px) 55vw, 100vw`, first image eager + high priority, alt falls back to product title); collection cards `sections/crooks-exhibit-log.liquid` L420–466 (widths 200–800, first card eager/high on collection pages, hover images alt="" as decorative); schema `snippets/crooks-schema-product.liquid` (first 5 `product.images` at 1600w; per-variant `variant.image` fallback to featured image); `snippets/meta-tags.liquid` L173–201.
- **Reaction module:** `snippets/crooks-reaction.liquid` renders the normal and reacted photos as real server-side `<img>` tags with srcset and alt, so reacted images placed there would be crawlable. Neither reactive product uses its reaction template on the live store (Puffa template suffix "", HydroCuff null). On the live theme both reaction templates point at placeholders; the unpublished "Puffa heat reaction" theme (last edit 14:24 today, outside this project) has the real heat-reacted Puffa photo in `product.reaction-puffa.json`. That work is in progress by someone else, so leave that theme alone.
- **Variant images:** colour products (Crooks Express, CRX Garms, CRXST★RZ, Pink Convict Hoodie V1/V2) have per-variant images; size-only products correctly share one. No action.
- **Image dimensions:** all square 1000–2048px except the Puffa (1122×1402, 4:5), which crops differently in square grid cards. No action until the Puffa is reshot.
- **Sitemap:** Shopify lists only the featured image per product, with alt as the caption, so the first image and its alt carry most of the image-search signal. Puffa caption is empty; the cap has no entry.
- **Collections:** no collection has an image set (all 9 in Admin). Collection pages show product cards, so this only affects collection-list blocks and share previews. P4.
